Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I contact MateMet support?

Email us at matemet.feedback@gmail.com. Please describe the issue and include your MateMet user ID and device details when relevant. Do not send your password or payment card details.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Check your internet connection, make sure you are using the latest version of MateMet, and try again. If the issue continues, contact support with your user ID, device model and a description of the error.

How do I manage or cancel an Apple subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select MateMet and follow the on-screen instructions. For billing assistance, visit Apple Support.

How can I report or block another user?

Use the report or block controls available in the app where supported. You can also email our support team with the user's ID and a clear description of your concern.

How can I request account deletion?

Email matemet.feedback@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. Include your MateMet user ID so we can identify the account. We may ask you to complete an account ownership verification step.
